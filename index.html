Introduction

Introduction to E-commerce in Papua New Guinea.

What is E-commerce?

E-commerce is the buying and selling of products and services through digital platforms.

In Papua New Guinea, online platforms can provide businesses and customers with new ways to communicate, promote products and complete transactions.

The growth of digital platforms, online shopping and electronic payments is helping to shape the development of e-commerce in PNG.

Types of E-commerce

Different types of e-commerce describe who is buying and who is selling.

B2B

B2B - Business to Business

B2B e-commerce involves transactions between one business and another business.

For example, a business may purchase products or services from another company through an online platform.

B2C

B2C - Business to Consumer

B2C e-commerce involves businesses selling products or services directly to customers.

Online stores are an example of business-to-consumer e-commerce.

C2C

C2C - Consumer to Consumer

C2C e-commerce involves one consumer selling products or services to another consumer.

Online marketplaces can allow consumers to communicate and trade with each other.

C2B

C2B - Consumer to Business

C2B e-commerce occurs when an individual provides products or services to a business.

For example, an individual may offer digital services to a company.

Benefits

How e-commerce can support customers and businesses in Papua New Guinea.

Benefits of E-commerce

E-commerce provides businesses and customers with convenient ways to buy and sell products and services.

Businesses can reach a wider market, communicate with customers more easily and potentially reduce some operating costs.

  • Wider customer reach
  • Convenient shopping
  • Business growth opportunities

Wider Customer Reach

E-commerce can allow businesses to reach customers outside their immediate physical location.

Convenient Shopping

Customers can search for products and communicate with sellers through digital platforms.

Business Growth Opportunities

Digital platforms can help businesses promote products and communicate with potential customers.

Trends

Key trends influencing online buying and selling in PNG.

Online Shopping

Online shopping allows customers to search for products and services using digital platforms.

Customers can communicate with sellers and place orders without always needing to visit a physical store.

  • Search for products online
  • Communicate with sellers
  • Place orders digitally

Digital Payments

Digital payments allow customers and businesses to complete transactions electronically.

Electronic payment methods can support convenient online transactions.

  • Electronic transactions
  • Faster payments
  • Convenient payment methods

Social Media

Social media is increasingly used by businesses to advertise products and communicate with customers.

Businesses can use social media platforms to display products and reach potential customers.

  • Product promotion
  • Customer communication
  • Online advertising

Mobile Commerce

Mobile commerce allows customers to browse, purchase products and communicate with businesses using mobile devices such as smartphones.

Mobile devices are becoming an important way for customers to access online shopping services.

  • Shopping using smartphones
  • Mobile access to online stores
  • Convenient digital transactions

Challenges

Some of the main challenges affecting e-commerce in Papua New Guinea.

Challenges of E-commerce

E-commerce in Papua New Guinea faces several challenges, including limited internet access, cybersecurity concerns and digital skills gaps.

  • Limited internet access
  • Cybersecurity concerns
  • Digital skills gaps

Delivery Challenges

Delivery can be challenging because of transportation costs, distance and infrastructure limitations.

These factors can affect how quickly products reach customers in different parts of Papua New Guinea.

  • Transportation costs
  • Long distances
  • Infrastructure limitations

Payment Barriers

Some customers may have limited access to digital payment services, which can make online transactions more difficult.

Limited payment options can affect the ability of customers and businesses to complete online purchases.

  • Limited payment options
  • Access to digital payment services
  • Transaction difficulties

Digital Skills

Digital skills are important for customers and businesses using e-commerce platforms.

A lack of digital skills can make it harder for people to use online services effectively.

  • Limited digital knowledge
  • Difficulty using online platforms
  • Need for digital training

Conclusion

Overall view of e-commerce in Papua New Guinea.

Conclusion

E-commerce provides opportunities for businesses and customers in Papua New Guinea through online shopping, digital payments and social media.

It can help businesses reach customers beyond their physical locations and provide convenient ways to promote and sell products.

At the same time, challenges such as limited internet access, cybersecurity concerns, digital skills gaps and delivery difficulties can affect online business.

Continued improvement in digital access, skills and supporting infrastructure can help the e-commerce environment develop further.
